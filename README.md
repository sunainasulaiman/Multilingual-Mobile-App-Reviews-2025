# 📱 Multilingual Mobile App Reviews 2025 – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes multilingual mobile app reviews from 2025 to understand **app performance, user satisfaction, review trends, customer engagement, and user behavior**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall app performance
- Understand customer rating patterns
- Identify highly rated and lower-rated apps
- Analyze review volume and trends
- Understand user behavior across languages, countries, and devices
- Analyze verified purchase reviews
- Identify important customer feedback patterns
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations

---

📊 Dashboard Pages
 Power BI Dashboard Development
The final Power BI report was designed with five analytical pages, each having a specific purpose by page navigation.

 Page 1 – Executive Analysis
The Executive Analysis page provides a high-level overview of the entire dataset.
It focuses on important KPIs such as:
•	Total Reviews 
•	Average Rating 
•	Total Helpful Votes 
•	Verified Purchase % 
•	Number of Apps 
•	Average User Age 
Charts and visuals provide an overall understanding of:
•	Reviews by app – Clustered bar chart
•	Reviews by category-Clustered bar chart
•	Reviews over month-Area Chart

This page is designed for users who want to understand the overall performance of the mobile applications at a glance.


Page 2 – App & Rating Analysis

This page focuses specifically on app performance and rating.
Charts and visuals provide an overall understanding of:
•	Average rating by app  – Clustered bar chart
•	Average rating by app category – Clustered bar chart
•	Reviews by app category – Clustered bar chart
•	Rating distribution  – Pie chart
•	Total helpful votes by app – Clustered bar chart


 Page 3 – User & Review Analysis
 
This page examines the characteristics and behaviour of users who submitted reviews.
The analysis includes areas such as:
•	Average User age  – Measure
•	Reviews by language – Clustered column chart
•	Reviews by country – Map
•	Reviews by device type – Donut chart 
•	Reviews by gender – Pie chart


 Page 4 – App Details / Drill-Through
 
A dedicated App Details page was created using Power BI's Drill-through functionality.
This allows users to select a particular app from another page and navigate to a detailed view of that app.
The page dynamically displays information related to the selected application.
The Selected App measure provides a dynamic title such as: Selected Name from other pages.
Ex:"Twitter”
depending on the user's selection.The page includes KPI cards and different charts

🔍 Key Insights
⭐ App Performance

Reddit and Pinterest have the highest average ratings among the apps analyzed, indicating stronger user satisfaction compared with other apps in the dataset.

⭐ Rating Performance

The overall average rating is approximately 3.02 out of 5, suggesting moderate overall customer satisfaction and opportunities for improvement.

👍 Customer Engagement

The dataset contains approximately 1.55 million helpful votes, indicating strong interaction with user reviews.

✅ Verified Reviews

Approximately 77.13% of reviews are from verified purchases, providing a strong base of verified customer feedback.

🌍 Multilingual Feedback

The dataset contains reviews across multiple languages and countries, providing an opportunity to understand regional and multilingual customer experiences.

💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. Benchmark High-Rated Apps

Analyze successful apps such as Reddit and Pinterest to identify features and user-experience practices associated with higher ratings.

2. Improve Low-Rated Experiences

Analyze 1–2 star reviews to identify recurring complaints and prioritize improvements.

3. Use Helpful Reviews

Give greater attention to highly helpful reviews because they can highlight issues and experiences that are important to a wider group of users.

4. Strengthen Localization

Use language and country-level insights to improve localized features, communication, and customer support.

5. Monitor Customer Satisfaction

Track ratings and review trends regularly to identify changes in customer satisfaction and evaluate the impact of product improvements.

6. Focus on Verified Feedback

Use verified-purchase reviews as an important input when evaluating product quality and customer experience.
